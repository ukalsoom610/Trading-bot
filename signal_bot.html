Strategy Performance

real outcomes adjusting real weight — not a black box

When a Tracked Signal or an AI Brain paper trade actually resolves — price genuinely hits its take-profit or stop-loss — every strategy that voted for that call gets credited with a win or blamed for a loss. A strategy with a real track record of being right earns a bit more influence (capped at 1.3× its base weight); one that keeps missing earns a bit less (floored at 0.7×). It takes at least 3 resolved trades before any adjustment happens, and this resets when you close the app.

Rule-based confluence signals from public, documented strategies — not financial advice, not a guarantee of accuracy. Futures trading is leveraged and can lose more than your initial margin. Long/short percentages reflect how strongly the strategies currently lean each way, not a statistical probability of profit — both can be low at once when signals are genuinely unclear. The "next N minutes" horizon reflects the candle timeframe the indicators are computed on, not a literal price forecast. Entry is shown as a suggested LIMIT zone — a modest pullback toward the mean — instead of the current market price, so you're not chasing the move; the trade-off is it may not fill if price runs without any pullback at all. Stop-loss/take-profit distances are scaled to the horizon you selected, so a 15-minute signal isn't carrying a multi-day target. None of this changes the underlying win rate — it's better risk placement, not a more accurate prediction. Runs entirely in your browser: fetches public data directly from the exchange you pick, computes everything locally, sends nothing anywhere, needs no account or API key. No trades are placed automatically.
